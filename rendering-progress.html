3D Rendering

Final Solar System Output

This is the final output and view for the entire Solar System project, as well as the tools used to achieve the final result. The Solar System includes accurate revolution speeds of the planets (timelapsed), as well as some asteroids and a space ship.

Blender 5.1 The main 3D modeling and rendering software used for the Solar System and each planet, as well as their animations.
EEVEE The rendering engine used in Blender to support rendering effects like lighting and shading.
Sketchfab Original source for the Viltrumite ship model used in the Solar System.

Interactive Feature

Calculate Your Age on Other Planets

Enter your Earth age and the page will show your equivalent age across the solar system.

The conversion uses each planet's orbital period relative to Earth.
