Final Output and Weekly Progress

This is the final output for the Rotoscope Project, along with what tools were used and steps taken to achieve the final result. The original video is Chika Dance, covered by Flaming Centurion. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UNDD9x1uUXQ&t=63s

Adobe After Effects 2023 A tool in AE called "Rotobrush" was used to separate the actor from the background, as well as applying different colors and its transitions.
Capcut Capcut was used for editing elements such as the addition of lyrics in the video and the comparison between the original source video.
